Elena's Background

Designing the Blueprint for High-Growth Workforces

As a former VP of People and Head of Talent at scaling technology startups, I witnessed first-hand the chaos that occurs when a team expands from 20 to 100+ members. The horizontal structures that succeeded in the initial phases quickly lead to reporting overlaps, communication fatigue, and key talent attrition.

I founded Vanguard Advisory to deliver high-impact, quantitative organizational design consulting directly to founders, CEOs, and investors. By operating as a single-person freelance specialist, I bypass the typical agency bloat and deliver custom-tailored, data-backed operational structures directly into your growth framework.

"Structure dictates behavior. Rather than attempting to resolve organizational friction with superficial team-building or generic training, I focus on building the structural, incentive, and communication systems that drive execution."
