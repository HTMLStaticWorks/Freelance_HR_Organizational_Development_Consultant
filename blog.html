Insights FAQ

Knowledge Base & Frameworks

Answers regarding our research methodology, framework redistribution licenses, and advisory application.

How often do you publish new organizational design frameworks?

I publish executive insights and strategic templates monthly. Every framework, diagnostic benchmark, and case analysis published in our library is distilled directly from active advisory engagements with VC-backed scale-ups and mid-market organizations.

Can we share your diagnostic templates and articles with our internal management?

Yes, sharing is highly encouraged. All organizational layout charts, compensation frameworks, and decision charters (such as DACI models) published in Vanguard Insights are free to distribute under Creative Commons licensing. I only ask that you maintain attribution back to Vanguard Advisory.

Do you accept guest articles or co-authored research from other people-strategy leaders?

I rarely publish external guest content. My publication focuses strictly on primary-source, quantitative data findings from our actual client diagnostic audits. If you have unique workforce research or data-backed org restructuring metrics, feel free to pitch our editorial board.

How can we apply these insights to our company's specific growth stage?

Each article highlights specific target organizations (e.g., Series A headcount scaling vs. Series B/C restructuring). For customized organizational audits or to evaluate your exact structural charts, we suggest launching our interactive Strategic Diagnostic Wizard or contacting my advisory desk directly.
