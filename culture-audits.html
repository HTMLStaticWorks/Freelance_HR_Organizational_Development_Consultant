Culture Audits & Sentiment Diagnostics

Using data, surveys, and structured qualitative interviews to isolate management bottlenecks and retention hazards.

Measuring Psychological Safety & Finding Culture Friction

"Company Culture" is not a vague sentiment; it is a measurable set of operational habits, trust dynamics, and coordination pathways. When culture breaks down, execution speed drops, internal politics increase, and top performers quietly start interviewing elsewhere.

Our Culture Audit is a quantitative and qualitative diagnostic wrapper. We bypass sugar-coated feedback loops, giving leadership teams an objective, data-backed assessment of psychological safety, alignment thresholds, and systemic retention risks.

Our Audit Methodology

01

Custom Diagnostic Surveys

Designing anonymous, benchmarked surveys that target communication quality, psychological safety limits, and alignment with corporate strategy.

02

Qualitative Focus Groups

Facilitating structured, non-retaliatory focus group discussions and 1-on-1 interviews with key department leaders and staff.

03

Operational Friction Mapping

Isolating where project bottlenecks occur due to lack of coordination, conflicting goals, or management failure.
