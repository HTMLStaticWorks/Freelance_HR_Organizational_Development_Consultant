Scale-up Challenges

Critical Scale Milestones

Startups face unique structural friction points at distinct team sizes. We configure custom processes to clear these hurdles.

Team Size: 15 - 30

Communication Silos

Building the first layers of middle management and documentation without adding excessive red tape or slow consensus loops.

Team Size: 30 - 80

Culture Friction Audits

Detecting psychological safety drops and role confusion as the core founding team transitions to specialized departments.

Team Size: 80 - 150+

Structure Restructuring

Formalizing division designs, logical reporting metrics, executive coaching lines, and transparent OKR alignment mechanisms.
